# audio/oddity

Night oddities, generated by `python3 audio/tools/gen_amb.py odd_`
(deterministic; the voices come from CC0 recordings, so run
`fetch_sources.py` first, everything else is synthesis). 48 kHz OGG Vorbis q6.
Unsettling but quiet: nothing has a sharp attack and nothing is normalised
to full scale (levels below are integrated loudness).

No speech synthesiser is installed (no espeak-ng/flite), so the voices are
real people made wordless. The station's reader is a man reading a story
(`voice_reader`), cut into syllables that are each played **backwards**,
slowed a touch and re-sequenced into calm reading groups, with a faint second
take a hair behind it (two mouths, one voice). The whispers are real
whispering (`whisper_ind`, `whisper_four`), also reversed. Timbre, breath and
cadence stay human; no words survive, and the radio chain (band-limit,
fading, wobble, static) buries them further. Swap in a real recorded voice
through the same radio chain if you want words.
