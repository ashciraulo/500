# audio/amb

Generated by `python3 audio/tools/gen_amb.py` (deterministic) from CC0 field
recordings fetched by `python3 audio/tools/fetch_sources.py` into
`build/sources/` (see [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md); keys below), plus synthesis.
48 kHz OGG Vorbis q6.

- **Beds** (`amb_<place>_<day|night>`): stereo, 60-120 s seamless loops, no
  fades, -24 LUFS. Each is layered in numpy: a woven base texture (random
  chunks of a recording joined with equal-power crossfades, tail crossfaded
  over the head) and/or circular synthesis, with recorded or synthesised events
  scattered at random times, gains, pans and distances and wrapped around the
  loop end. The few bird/clatter peaks are tucked under -2 dBFS by a
  circular (seam-safe) limiter. The loop seam is verified numerically on every
  build (jump across the join must look like any other sample step).
- **One-shots**: mono, 3D-positioned in game, peak -1 dBTP. The `_loop`
  one-shots (crossing bells, pedestrian signals) are mono seamless loops at
  -1 dBTP for placing on an actual crossing.

Notes
- Gulls are synthesised silver gulls: no CC0 recording of the Australian
  species was found, and European herring gulls (the usual library gull) sound
  wrong for Perth.
- Recordings from elsewhere in Australia (Adelaide crickets, Brisbane traffic,
  Melbourne bar, Sydney magpies) and a few generic non-Australian ones (US
  freeway, NYC ferry engine, Coney Island lapping, Canadian lawnmower, Balkan
  dog) are used only where the content has no regional character, filtered
  and/or placed at a distance.
