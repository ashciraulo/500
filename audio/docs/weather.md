# audio/weather

Generated by `python3 audio/tools/gen_weather.py` (deterministic). 48 kHz OGG Vorbis q6.
Beds are stereo seamless loops. **Normalisation:** light rain, windscreen, wind bed and cicadas at
-24 LUFS ("amb"); heavy rain beds and wind buffeting at -20 LUFS so heavy stays ~4 dB louder than
light. Thunder and gusts are stereo one-shots, drips mono one-shots, all peak -1 dBTP.
