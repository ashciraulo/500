# audio/home

Generated by `python3 audio/tools/gen_home.py` (deterministic; imports instruments from gen_ui.py and
the rain drop field from gen_weather.py; the cat's meows and purr are CC0 recordings, so run
`fetch_sources.py` first). 48 kHz OGG Vorbis q6. Small, dry room character (short,
lightly mixed reflection). Point sources mono; `home_rain_windows` and `home_day_ends` stereo.
One-shots peak -1 dBTP; room-tone style loops (fridge, aircon, record crackle/run-out, intercom
static, purr, rain on windows) at -24 LUFS; the clock loop peaks at -1 dBTP.

## The studio's instruments

Picked up and played a little in bedroom 2. Each is a variant set: play
`Audio.variant("home/home_studio_dove_strum")` (or `_tele`, `_jbass`) at the
instrument, so a second go is a different take. Made by `gen_studio.py`
(FluidSynth, then a small bedroom's reverb).

| home_studio_jbass_01..03 | no | A walking line on the Jazz Bass through the practice amp (6-7 s) |

## The record player

The record player (scripts/world/record_crate.gd) asks for home/home_record_needle,
an alias of home_record_needle_drop. Audio.hooks adds home_record_crackle under
the record while it plays (on the record's own Music bus, 20 dB down) and the
needle lift when it stops.
